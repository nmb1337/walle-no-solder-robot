# Purchasing status

The old CoreS3, 70 mm wheel, PLA and 5 V / 3 A shopping list is obsolete. Do
not add or purchase from it.

The authoritative no-solder purchase list, exact dimensions, quantity, search
words and seller-confirmation message are in [../docs/BOM.md](../docs/BOM.md).
The V2 design needs two separate regulated rails: `5 V / 10 A` for servos and
logic, and `6.0 V / 3 A` for the 6 V N20 motors.

The Taobao cart is intentionally not treated as verified. Before any item is
added, compare its product page with `docs/BOM.md`, especially the wheel bore
and width, motor stall current, real battery dimensions and all pre-crimped
connectors.
